Political Sports Cards is a nonpartisan civic education platform. Every score is generated through automated, equal-weighted formulas applied to verified public government data (e.g. Congress.gov, FEC.gov, state ethics registries) — never opinion, editorial judgment, or paid influence. PSC does not endorse, oppose, or accept advertising, sponsorships, or contributions from any political candidate, campaign, party, or PAC. The archetype cards shown on this pre-launch site are illustrative placeholders; real candidate cards and sourced data debut at launch.
